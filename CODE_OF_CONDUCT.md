# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Omnisnap to be a welcoming and respectful project for everyone who
takes part, whatever their background, identity or experience level.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate, especially when you disagree.
- Critique code, measurements and ideas, not people. Give and receive feedback
  constructively.
- Assume good intent, and ask for clarification before assuming the worst.
- Be honest about what you know, what you don't, and how your contribution was
  made.
- Respect the maintainer's time and their decisions about the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination of any kind.
- Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Publishing other people's private information without their explicit
  permission.
- Trolling, spamming, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Repeatedly pressuring the maintainer to review, accept, or prioritize
  contributions.
- Misrepresenting the authorship of a contribution, including undisclosed use
  of AI tools (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#ai-assisted-contributions)).

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all of Omnisnap's spaces: the repository,
issues, pull requests, Discussions, and any other channel the project runs. It
also applies when someone represents Omnisnap in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it with GitHub's
**Report content** option, in the "…" menu of the comment, issue, pull request
or discussion:

- **Report to repository admins** sends it privately to the maintainer.
- **Report abuse** sends it to GitHub's own moderators. Use this one if the
  report concerns the maintainer, or if you'd rather not go through them.

Reports are reviewed as soon as possible and handled as confidentially as
possible.

## Enforcement

Most problems are misunderstandings, and the response starts with the lightest
step that resolves them:

1. **A friendly note**, pointing out what went wrong and how to put it right.
2. **A private warning**, explaining clearly what crossed the line, if it
   happens again.
3. **A temporary pause** from interacting with the project, if the behaviour
   continues after a warning.
4. **A permanent ban**, only for serious or repeated violations.

Comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this
Code of Conduct may be edited, hidden or removed. Anyone affected by a decision
may ask the maintainer to reconsider it.
